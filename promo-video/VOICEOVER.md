# Inboxed English voiceover

Voice: Kokoro `af_heart` (American English). Calm delivery, one take per scene.

## The inbox problem (0–8s)

Newsletters, receipts, sign-in codes. Your inbox fills up fast, and the messages that matter get buried.

## The Inboxed approach (8–14s)

Inboxed sorts the noise into clear suggestions. You decide what stays, and what goes.

## Scan and workspace (14–23s)

Start with a scan. Inboxed groups your mail into categories. Open any message to read it. Your mailbox stays unchanged.

## Review and apply (23–33s)

Review the suggestions. Change a class, approve messages, and preview every action. Apply when you're ready. Missing folders are created automatically.

## Unsubscribe (33–42s)

Stop the clutter coming back. Find mailing lists, read their emails, and review supported unsubscribe requests before sending.

## Classes and automation (42–50s)

Create your own classes. Choose cleanup rules and confidence thresholds. Turn on automation for selected classes.

## Command line (50–59s)

Prefer the command line? Scan, preview changes with a dry run, and inspect your settings, right from the terminal.

## Local history and privacy (59–70s)

Your run history stays on your computer. Revisit past activity, protect saved runs with a password, and choose a compatible local model.

## End card (70–77s)

Make room for what matters. Find Inboxed at inboxed dot F Y I.
