You stay responsible for your mailbox

Inboxed suggests ways to organize email. AI classifications can be wrong. Review proposed changes, understand the permissions you grant, and make backups where needed before applying actions or enabling automation.

1. The project

Inboxed is an open-source email organizer maintained by Kryštof Marval and contributors. It runs on your computer and connects to a configured IMAP provider. This website provides project information, documentation links, and privacy disclosures.

Third-party dependencies and models have their own licenses.

3. Responsible use

Connect only mailboxes you own or are authorized to manage. Comply with applicable law and your mail provider’s terms. Do not use Inboxed to access another person’s messages without permission, misuse credentials, evade provider limits, or interfere with services.

You are responsible for your local installation, device security, account credentials, configuration, data backups, and any modifications or extensions you run. Keep local models and dependencies updated as appropriate for your environment.

4. AI suggestions and mailbox actions

Model output is a suggestion, not a guarantee of correct classification or a substitute for your judgment. Review the subject, sender, preview, category and proposed action before approval. A scan saves proposals without applying mailbox changes.

With write access, applying actions can change labels, archive messages, or move them to Trash. Automation can apply eligible actions without individual review when you explicitly configure and invoke it. These operations can affect important messages. Provider retention policies may permanently delete items left in Trash. Inboxed does not provide a universal undo mechanism.

5. Third-party services

Your IMAP provider, GitHub, Cloudflare, Ollama, and model or dependency providers operate independently. Their terms, policies, availability, and limits apply to their services. Mailbox access depends on valid IMAP credentials, your provider’s authentication requirements, and any applicable administrator restrictions.

Service changes, outages, revoked credentials, or connection limits may affect functionality. Use of external services can carry charges or requirements set by those providers.

6. Privacy

The Inboxed privacy policy explains the data accessed by the app, local AI processing, saved history, mailbox credentials, and how to revoke access or delete local data. Review it before connecting a mailbox. Do not post message contents, tokens, passwords, or local database files in public support issues.

For questions or support, use the Inboxed issue tracker. For a sensitive concern, first ask for a private contact route without posting sensitive information.
